# Explore how IW works

You do not need to read these guides before making a world. They are here when you want to understand a feature, compare approaches or find out why something behaved unexpectedly. Your AI can also use them to help you.

## Design your world

- [Craft](Craft.md): Build an engaging opening, memorable characters and the tone you want.
- [Recipes](Recipes.md): See ways to combine features, from optional story settings to purchases and unlocks.
- [Character and data banks](Portable-Banks.md): Save characters and other reusable parts to bring into future worlds.

## Understand the features

- [Trackers](Trackers.md): Remember money, relationships, inventory and other changing details.
- [Triggers](Triggers.md): Make something happen when a condition is met.
- [Trigger capability catalog](Trigger-Catalog.md): Every identified trigger condition and effect in one table.
- [PawScript](PawScript.md): Learn to read and change game data with scripts and expressions.
- [Capabilities](Capabilities.md): Explore Skills, character information, images, timing and other IW behavior.

## Check the details

- [Schema guide](Schema.md): Find the technical structure of an IW export.
- [Default Evaluation](Default-Evaluation.txt): Read IW's supplied instructions for judging player actions.
- [Storyteller models](Current-AI-Profiles.json): Model IDs, the real-world model behind each, which are current, and typical cost per turn.

## Current behavior, limits and editor help

- [Notable findings](Notable-Findings.md): The behavior most likely to surprise you or break a world, in plain language, and what remains uncertain.
- [Estimating cost](Cost.md): What a turn costs on each model, why players' costs differ, and how to estimate a world's cost.
- [Editor and UI reference](Editor-UI.md): Labels, menus, visibility, import checks, live copies and troubleshooting.
- [Consolidated behavior](Verified-Behavior.md): What has been tested in IW and the results, with the kit's compatibility baseline and evidence labels.

The validator, import audit, story extractor, plot compiler and sorter are documented in [tools/Usage.md](../tools/Usage.md).
