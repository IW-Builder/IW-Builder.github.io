IW Cost Exporter

Downloads your whole Infinite Worlds credit history (every turn, its cost breakdown, model, world and timestamp) as a spreadsheet-ready CSV and a JSON file.

Install

Drag this button onto your browser's bookmarks bar:

IW Cost Export

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac) to show it. Clicking the button here does nothing; it only works from your bookmarks on the Infinite Worlds site.

Can't drag it? Add it by hand

Copy the code, create a new bookmark named "IW Cost Export", and paste the code into its URL or address field.

Use

  1. Open infiniteworlds.app and sign in.
  2. Click your credit balance at the top right, then Credit activity.
  3. With that list open, click the IW Cost Export bookmark. A status box appears in the bottom-right corner and counts the transactions as it collects them.
  4. When it says Done, two files download.

What you get

.csvOne row per transaction, newest first. Columns include datetime (UTC), world_name, turn_number, ai_profile, total_cost, new_credit_balance and the input/output cost breakdown. .jsonThe same records unflattened, for scripts.

It only reads your own credit history through the connection the site already has open. It sends nothing anywhere else and doesn't take game actions.

A large history takes a minute or two. Leave the tab open until it finishes; Stop and save in the status box stops early and downloads what it has so far. If IW is slow it retries on its own; if it still can't get a reply it saves what it has and says why.
