Dial It Up

Aurelia Cove, a college and resort town, one week after the Loom shattered. You hold the Dial Shard, a brass chronometer that rewrites bodies, minds and the rules of a room. Three rivals hold Shards of their own. This page shows how the story unfolds and how every mechanic works. In a hurry? Try the 30-second quick start.

Story flow

How the story unfolds

Between beats the game is wide open: you decide what to change, who to chase and who to cross. The story of the Loom moves forward in four beats. Each one opens after three more firings of your Shard or fifteen more turns, whichever comes first, so a bold player reaches them sooner.

Every turn

What happens in one turn

The storyteller writes the scene, and the game keeps the numbers. You never need to track charges or cooldowns yourself.

1 · Read the status

The 🕰️ Status banner after each turn shows your charges, when the next one is back, the Resonance for your next turn and the side effect an off-tune shot would carry.

2 · Act

Fire the Shard, siphon, scan a rival, flirt, plot or explore. Anything goes.

3 · The story

The change lands in this same turn, clean or with its side effect. Rivals act if the game called on one.

4 · The game settles up

Spends the charge, ticks every cooldown, may wake a rival or a Loom beat, then rolls next turn's Resonance and side effect.

Mechanics · your Shard

The Dial Shard

  1. Two channels. Each firing spends one. A spent channel comes back after five full turns. The two recharge separately, so you can fire twice in a row and then wait.
  2. Resonance. Every turn the Shard hums on one of five Resonances. An edit in that Resonance's domain lands clean and precise.
  3. Side effects. Fire outside the Resonance, or during Wild Surge, and the edit lands with a side effect. The HUD shows which one before you fire, so you can use it on purpose.
  4. Dry clicks. With no charge ready, the chronometer clicks dry or sparks harmlessly. Nothing is spent.

Your Shard Mastery skill can steady it: each turn there's a one-in-ten chance per level that the side effect is "None" and an off-tune edit lands exactly as intended. Caspian starts at 4 (40%), Margo at 3 (30%).

Mechanics · tricks

Siphon and scan

Siphon

Deliberately draw resonance from a climax, intense intimate contact, or another wielder's artifact. It takes a turn off both recharging channels. With both channels already full, the excess pushes next turn into Wild Surge instead.

Once every four turns. The HUD shows when it's available again.

Scan

Use the Shard to read someone, without firing. You learn whether they hold a Shard, its domain and restriction, how ready it is, and any changes already on them. Scanning a rival is how you see her cooldown and, for Odette, how close she is to backfiring.

When a rival acts

  1. Provoke her and she answers. Taunt or dare a rival, mock her Shard, invite her to use it on you, or aim yours at her. If her Shard is ready, she uses it.
  2. Otherwise it's a roll. From turn 2, each turn has a 35% chance that one ready rival who is in the scene makes a move.
  3. One at a time. After any rival acts, no rival acts on her own for three turns. In practice that's about one turn in five.
  4. You stay in control. A rival can aim her power at you and you'll feel it, but the game never decides what your character says or does.
Mechanics · the world

What the world remembers

The Reality & Transformation Ledger

Visible to you. It keeps up to six active local laws (where they apply, the rule, temporary or permanent) and one entry for every person whose body or mind has been changed.

Two kinds of edits

Mind-Revealed: they know they changed, and feel the shock or the thrill of it. Normalized: they and everyone around them believe it was always this way. Only Shard wielders notice the difference.

Messages you'll see
