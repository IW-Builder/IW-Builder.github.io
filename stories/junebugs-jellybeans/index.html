The one idea

How a bean works

Every bean has a color and a pattern. Which color is which flavor, and which pattern is which zone, is dealt fresh each game. That mapping is the key, and working it out by tasting is the puzzle (or pick Labeled shelves and skip it).

Color

is the flavor

What kind of change: Lush, Sleek, Glam and so on. Ten of the flavors are in stock each game, one per color.

Pattern

is the zone

Where on the body it lands. Six patterns, six zones. A change never leaves its zone.

Center

is the twist

One center applies to every bean bitten in a turn. Soft, the most common, adds nothing.

ZoneCovers
CrownHair, face, ears, voice
BustChest, shoulders, arms, hands
CurveWaist, hips, rear
StrideLegs, feet, height, tail
SkinThe surface everywhere: color, texture, markings
MindManner, habits, urges, speech

Levels

1A hint. You would notice if you were looking.
2Unmistakable.
3Dramatic, and it has set.
  1. Same flavor again in the same zone: one level deeper, up to 3.
  2. A different flavor in that zone: it replaces the old one at level 1. A level 3 change has set, so other flavors bounce off it.
  3. A clear bean of that zone's pattern: undoes one level, at any level. From level 1 the change is gone.

Changes last until a clear bean undoes them. Each person has one slot per zone, so one person can carry up to six changes at once. The Bean Ledger tracker lists them all.

Your turn

What you can do

Type anything. These are the moves the game knows by name. Each turn also offers three suggestions, one of each kind.

With a bean

  • Bite. Eat one bean. Its color and pattern decide the change.
  • Feed. Offer someone a bean. Whoever eats it changes.
  • Pair. Two beans in one bite. Some colors pair into something special; otherwise both beans work.
  • Slip. Hide a bean in food or drink. The eater notices the change but not the cause.
  • Kiss-pass. Bite a bean and kiss someone: it changes both of you.
  • Roulette. Everyone grabs a bean without looking. Each gets one of the turn's lucky dips.
  • Clear. A clear bean undoes one level of a change in its pattern's zone.
  • Special. One rare bean a day on the chalkboard. Its effect lasts until morning.

In the shop

  • Serve. Sell a customer what they asked for, recommend something else, test it first, or say no.
  • Sample tray. Put out free samples: walk-ins take lucky dips.
  • Window display. Feature one bean in the window and see who it brings in.
  • Gift bag. Sell beans to take home. Where they end up is out of your hands.
  • Bake. Bake beans into fudge or cupcakes for a party or the counter.
  • Dulcie in charge. Leave Dulcie minding the counter and go out into town.

People and the town

  • Regulars. Your regulars each want something. Find out what.
  • Town. Beans turn up around town from somewhere else. Follow the trail if you like.
  • Returns. An unhappy customer can be fixed with a clear bean, won over, given a free bean, or talked into going further.
  • Order. A customer has ordered something for a set day. Have it ready.
  • Dulcie. Ask Dulcie to taste-test one. She is not supposed to.

Lucky dips. Anyone who takes a bean without choosing (a blind grab, a free sample, roulette, a bean Dulcie holds out) gets a random one. Dulcie reads its color and pattern aloud first, which makes dips a cheap way to learn the key.

The twist

Centers

The turn's center applies to every bean bitten that turn, by anyone. It applies once to a pairing and not at all to a clear bean.

CenterWhat it addsHow often
SoftPlain: the change arrives at once, exactly as the bean says.Common
FizzyThe change flares a step stronger whenever the eater is excited or embarrassed, then settles back.Now and then
StickyThe nearest person catches a faint, level 1 trace of the same change too.Now and then
Slow-meltThe change creeps in over the next few turns, and other people notice it before the eater does.Now and then
SourThe change comes with a sharp matching mood that lasts the rest of the scene.Now and then
SweetThe eater craves that flavor again: drawn back to that color and restless until the change reaches level 3, without quite realizing why; the craving ends at level 3 or when the zone is cleared.Now and then
GlazedThe eater is proud of the change and wants it seen: shows it off, dresses to display it and fishes for looks and compliments for the rest of the day.Now and then
Running the place

The shop's days

Customers

Two a day to start. One more at word of mouth 2, another at 4, and one extra on Saturday, market day. They know what change they want, not which bean does it. Bolder customers arrive as the shop's name spreads.

Regulars

Each location has three. Every regular privately wants one flavor on one zone. Work it out and sell it to them, and they pick a new want.

Returns

Someone you sold to may be back first thing on a later day. Only your own sales come back, and every return ends well: fixed with a clear bean, won over, made right with a free bean, or doubled down.

Special orders

Now and then someone orders a pairing you haven't found or a level 3 effect, due in three days. Miss one and the order book stays shut for two days.

Today's special

One rare bean a day on the chalkboard. Its effect is a mood or a moment, not a body change, and it is gone by morning.

Around town

About every other day a bean from somewhere else turns up in town. Those are not yours and the trail goes cold. They are color for the day.

Word of mouth (0 to 5, starts at 1)

What happensEffect
A customer leaves happyUp
A return ends won over or fixedUp
A special order is filledUp twice as much
You are rude to a customerDown

There is a cap on how much it can rise in one day, so a steady week beats one frantic afternoon.

Your assistant manager

Dulcie

Dulcie Marchpane came with the shop. She is eager, she has no power over any of this, and she never tells you the key. Her regard for you runs from 0 to 5. Being kind, being curious about her and standing up for her raise it. Mocking her lowers it.

RegardWhat you get
0 to 1Brittle company patter
2 to 3Warmer, more herself
4Candid. She confirms a right guess and will mind the counter alone
5Once a day she offers a second special

Pick one of the four owners, or change the name and description to make your own.

OptionChoices

AssortmentClassic (human flavors only), Everything (adds the fantastic shelf) or Custom (tick the flavors you want, at least three).
LabelsBlank (you work out the key by tasting) or Labeled (the shelf tags tell you).
PicturesWorld, Photographic, Painted or Anime, each as Slim (the default: good pictures, low cost) or High (best pictures, high cost). Or Basic, or Off for the lowest cost.
