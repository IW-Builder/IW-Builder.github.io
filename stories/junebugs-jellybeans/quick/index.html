One-minute start

Junebug's Jubilee Jellybeans

Your storefront turned into a candy shop overnight. The jellybeans change whoever eats them, customers are already at the door, and your assistant manager Dulcie knows what every bean does and isn't allowed to say. Adults only.

Every turn

1 · Look around

Who is in the shop and what do they want? The Shop Today tracker has the day's special and customers.

2 · Do anything

Eat a bean, sell one, feed one to someone, flirt, snoop. Type it, or pick one of the three suggestions.

3 · Watch it land

The change happens on the spot. The game keeps the ledger and the tasting card for you.

Reading a bean

Colorthe flavor: what kind of change
Patternthe zone: where it lands
Centerthe turn's twist

Which color is which flavor is different every game.

The big rules

  • Same flavor, same zone: one level deeper. 1 is a hint, 2 is unmistakable, 3 is dramatic.
  • A different flavor in that zone replaces the old one at level 1, unless the old one reached 3.
  • A clear bean of the zone's pattern undoes one level.
  • Two beans in one bite sometimes pair into something special.
Zones: CrownBustCurveStrideSkinMind

Good first moves

  • Eat one yourself and see what changed. That is one color and one pattern learned.
  • Sell the first customer your best guess. A happy sale spreads the word; a wrong one comes back as a return, and returns end well too.
  • Put out a sample tray or start a round of roulette. Dulcie reads each bean aloud, so you learn fast.
  • Be nice to Dulcie. The more she likes you, the more she lets slip.

Setting up

Pick an owner, then a Location (small town, city arcade or boardwalk). Assortment: Classic keeps every change human, Everything adds horns, tails and candy skin. Labels: Blank makes the key a puzzle, Labeled shows it from the start. Pictures: Slim is the low-cost default, High is the best and costs more, Off is cheapest.
