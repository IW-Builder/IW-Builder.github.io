30-second start

Dial It Up

You hold the Dial Shard, a brass chronometer that can rewrite anyone's body, mind or the rules of a room. It's a sun-soaked resort town, three rivals have Shards too, and nothing is off the table. Adults only.

Every turn

1 · Read the status

The 🕰️ Status banner after each turn: charges, when the next is back, the next Resonance and the side effect waiting for an off-tune shot.

2 · Do anything

Fire the Shard at someone, flirt, scheme, explore, or provoke a rival. Just describe it.

3 · Watch it land

The change happens right away. The game tracks charges and cooldowns for you.

The big rules

  • Two charges. Each takes five turns to come back.
  • Match the Resonance for a clean edit. Miss it and you get the side effect.
  • Siphon with a kiss or intimate touch to speed the recharge.
  • Scan anyone to read their Shard.

Who to watch

Odette Ashby, the flirty heiress. Reshapes bodies with a touch, and loves to show off.

Dr. Cosima Sterling, the cool professor. Rewrites the social rules and wants you as an ally, or under her pen.

Zoraia Quintero, the chaotic art student. Her camera splices ears, tails and gender swaps onto anyone she frames.

Taunt a rival and she'll answer with her Shard. Otherwise they make moves on their own now and then.

The world

Aurelia Cove: a college and luxury resort town. A week ago the Loom shattered in a cliffside lab and its Shards fused into everyday objects.

Getting started

Pick Caspian (steadier with the Shard) or Margo (smoother talker), or make your own. Choose a picture tier on the card: Lean (Thyrphax, fair cost, the default), Best (Storyloom, very expensive), or Off (cheapest). You start at a Friday pool party with both charges full. Try your first shot in tune with the Resonance, then see who notices. The bigger story of the Loom unfolds the more you use your Shard.
